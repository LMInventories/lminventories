Inventory report

Mid-tenancy

Midterm inspection

For mid-tenancy checks, we'll visit tenants at home, or collect keys to access when vacant, and take notes on the condition of the property, from signs of damage, damp or mould, to unauthorised pets, subletting or smoking. We'll take notes from the occupiers too.

Check out

At the end of the tenancy, we return, inventory report in hand, ready to compare conditions. Everything above and beyond fair wear and tear is reported with photographs, to help eliminate deposit disputes. We take meter readings, and return keys if we met the tenant at check in.
