Our practice

Two independent clerks. One standard.

L&M Inventories was founded by Robyn and Steven, two independent inventory clerks who kept running into the same problem: reports that were rushed, generic, or missing the detail that actually matters when a deposit dispute comes down to a single photograph.

We joined forces to build something better: a partnership built on consistency, so every client gets the same careful standard, whoever's holding the camera. We're not a call centre or a franchise. We still carry out the inspections ourselves.
